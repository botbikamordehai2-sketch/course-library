## What it is

Microsoft Copilot and Microsoft 365 Copilot are Microsoft's AI
assistant products integrated across Windows, the web, and the
Microsoft 365 app suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams,
SharePoint, OneDrive). The Microsoft Copilot Blog
(`techcommunity.microsoft.com/category/microsoft-copilot/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog`)
publishes a recurring monthly "What's New" changelog series as its main
cadence, plus per-product posts (e.g. a separate SharePoint-specific
"What's new in Copilot in SharePoint" series).

## Recent changes found this pass

- **Model choice expansion (2026):** Microsoft Copilot added
  third-party frontier models as selectable options alongside its own
  — the September 2026 update named OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude
  Sonnet 5.5; a further September 2026 expansion added Claude Opus 5.5,
  GPT-6 Sol, and Grok models. This means Copilot is explicitly
  multi-model/model-agnostic at the product level, not locked to one
  vendor's models.
- **Monthly changelog cadence:** separate "What's New in Microsoft
  Copilot" and "What's New in Microsoft 365 Copilot" series run in
  parallel most months (e.g. both an August and a "September" edition
  existed with overlapping dates in the search results — the blog's
  own post titles and dates didn't always align cleanly; flagged as
  UNVERIFIED exactly which post is the authoritative September edition
  rather than silently picking one).
