## Scope
Research and learning only. This public repository does not provide real-money trading instructions or broker execution workflows.

## Source status

## Learning sequence in the notes
1. Trading psychology and probabilistic thinking
2. Wyckoff foundations
3. Advanced market-structure concepts

## Research discipline
The source repeatedly stresses:
- advanced concepts must become short, testable rules;
- do not treat terminology as evidence;
- use historical testing before integrating any model into a research system;
- keep research separate from live execution.

## Public-safe takeaway
Treat every setup as a hypothesis to test, document assumptions, record invalidation conditions, and measure results over a sufficiently large sample. No live-money implementation is included here.
